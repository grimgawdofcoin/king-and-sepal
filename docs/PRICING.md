# Pricing (proposed, not decided)

Not in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` as a hard blocker yet, but flagged as open in
`BRAND.md`. This is a researched recommendation for the founder to confirm
or override — not a final decision.

## Revision (2026-07-21)
Founder feedback on the original $12–18/bar proposal: **too high**. Revised
down below, anchored against a wider spread of real per-bar prices rather
than subscription-box pricing, which was skewing the first estimate upward.

## Market reference points
- Approachable-gourmet single bars (Chuao Chocolatier, sold at Walmart
  among other retailers): **~$6/bar**.
- Ultra-premium single-origin bars (Dandelion Chocolate): **up to $20+/bar**
  — this is the top of the market, not a typical price, and reflects an
  established, well-known maker's pricing power.
- Craft/bean-to-bar generally (Fruition, Pump Street, Dick Taylor, etc.):
  **~$7–16/bar** individually, or $40–80 per DTC subscription shipment
  (4–6 bars) — subscription-box math was the main driver of the original,
  too-high estimate.

## Revised recommendation
- **Per-bar: $7–11**, not $12–18. This sits above mass-market candy
  (Kit Kat/Hershey's-tier) and above the $6 approachable-gourmet anchor,
  but well under ultra-premium single-origin pricing that an established
  maker can command and a brand-new one can't yet.
- Reasoning for coming down: King & Sepal has no track record yet — the
  melt mechanism is genuinely novel, but novelty alone doesn't support
  Dandelion-level pricing without the brand equity Dandelion has built.
  Pricing closer to Chuao's approachable-gourmet tier, with a modest
  premium for the melt feature, is more credible for a first release.
- **Hand-sell phase (30–50 units)**: price at the low end (~$7–8), or
  bundle a 3-flavor sampler, since the goal is word-of-mouth and feedback,
  not margin.
- **Drop 01**: once validated, there's room to move toward $11 or
  introduce a limited-run premium tier — but starting low and proving the
  product is the safer sequence for a brand-new name.

## What this doesn't account for yet
- Actual per-unit cost (cacao, lecithin, flavoring, packaging, shipping) —
  once a real cacao-butter quote and a packaging-direction decision land,
  margin can be checked against these numbers instead of just market
  comparables.
- Florida cottage food sales-tax handling on direct sales.
