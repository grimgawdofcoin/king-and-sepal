ampersand wordmark, premium not playful). **These are starting points for

## Prompt used
"Logo for 'King & Sepal', a small-batch craft cacao bar brand. Restrained,
minimalist, exclusive aesthetic inspired by the UK natural-perfume brand
Ffern. Plain confident wordmark typography, refined serif or sans-serif,
deep cocoa-brown and cream/ivory palette, small ampersand between 'King'
and 'Sepal', premium not playful."
