## About

We didn't set out to make a candy bar, or a creamer. We ended up with both,
in the same bar.

King & Sepal is cacao, formulated to do two things: hold its shape as
something you eat with your hands, and fully disappear into a hot drink —
coffee, tea, milk — when you want it to be that instead.

We make it in small runs. Not because we can't make more, but because we'd
rather get it right at a size we can actually stand behind. Drops are
numbered. When one sells out, the next one takes as long as it takes.

---

## How it works

**Eat it straight.** It's a bar. Unwrap it, eat it, done.

**Melt it in.** Drop a piece into a hot drink. It dissolves — no floating
film, no stirring for a minute waiting for it to give up. Just cacao,
becoming part of what you're drinking.

## FAQ (first draft — expand as real questions come in)

**Does it actually dissolve, or just melt?**
Dissolve. It's formulated to disperse fully into a hot drink, not sit on
top as an oil slick — that's the whole point of the recipe.

**Where do you ship?**
Florida only, for now. We're a home-kitchen operation under Florida's
cottage food law, which means direct, in-state sales while we're this
size. *(Confirm before publishing — see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` / cottage food
constraints in `BUSINESS_PLAN.md`.)*

**Why drops instead of always having it in stock?**
Small batches, made well, instead of a lot made to a lower bar. When a
drop sells out, we start the next one instead of stretching this one.
