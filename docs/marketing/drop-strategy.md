# Drop strategy — waitlist mechanics and cross-industry validation

Extends `BRAND.md`'s Ffern positioning with practical mechanics for
running an actual drop, plus validation that the scarcity/drop model works
well beyond fragrance specifically.

## The drop model isn't just a fragrance thing
Ffern is the aesthetic reference, but the underlying mechanic — small
batches, a waitlist, scheduled restocks instead of always-on inventory —
is used successfully across very different categories:
- **SKIMS**: weekly drops/restocks in batches of 20,000–200,000, designed
  to sell out fast; runs real waitlists (one product reportedly had a
  46,000-person waitlist).
- **Supreme**: small weekly batches, deliberately never restocked — scarcity
  by policy, not just by production limits.
- **Nike SNKRS**: app-based drop mechanic combining scarce supply with high
  demand.
- **Glossier**: restock windows treated as flash-sale events, among their
  highest-converting pages.
Source: https://queue-it.com/blog/product-drop/,
https://hello.quikly.com/blog/scarcity-marketing-examples.

Takeaway for King & Sepal: the mechanic (waitlist → numbered drop → sell
out → next drop) is well-proven at very different scales. At hand-sell/
Drop 01 size, the version of this that matters most is simple — a real
waitlist and a clear "this batch is numbered and won't be restocked the
same way" message — not the app/hype infrastructure SKIMS or Nike use.

## Waitlist tooling (for whenever Shopify setup happens)
Practical options once there's a store to attach a waitlist to — none set
up yet, this is just what's available:
- **LaunchList** (https://getlaunchlist.com/waitlist-for-ecommerce) —
  purpose-built for product drops. Hosted drop page, no theme
  customization needed, connects to Klaviyo/Shopify tags/webhooks,
  one-click "notify everyone" at drop time.
- **Waitlister** (https://waitlister.me/integrations/shopify) —
  Shopify-focused, 5-minute setup, exports to Shopify Email/Klaviyo/
  Mailchimp; cited 30–40% conversion from waitlist to sale.
- **Native Klaviyo + Shopify**: a blank Shopify page with an embedded
  Klaviyo signup form — more manual, but no extra app cost if already on
  Klaviyo for other email marketing.

For a first drop this small (30–50 units hand-sell, Drop 01 modest at
first), a simple Klaviyo-or-equivalent embedded form is probably enough —
the dedicated drop tools (LaunchList) earn their cost once drops are
frequent/large enough that manual list management becomes the bottleneck.
