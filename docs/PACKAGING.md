# Packaging direction

Not decided yet (see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`) — this is a set of concrete options
to choose between, not a decision made on the founder's behalf.

## What Ffern does, as the reference point
Compostable mushroom-fiber trays, textured paper tubes, original artwork per
batch. The packaging is treated as part of the ritual, not a wrapper — it's
part of why people talk about it.

## Candidate suppliers for a compostable bar wrapper/box
All of these serve small/custom orders and use compostable or recyclable
materials, found via web search — none contacted yet:

- **Good Start Packaging** (https://www.goodstartpackaging.com/candy-packaging/)
  — kraft paper, compostable cellulose film (NatureFlex), plant-based PLA;
  offers free samples and wholesale pricing. Good first stop for samples.
- **TIPA Eco Packaging** (https://tipa-corp.com/application/bar-wrapper/) —
  flexographic/digital printing up to 10 colors, packaging certified for
  home/industrial composting (TÜV AUSTRIA, BPI, and others). More premium
  print quality, likely higher MOQ.
- **Eco Custom Packaging** (https://ecocustompackaging.com/product/candy-bar-packaging/)
  — explicitly flexible MOQs "from startup ordering 100 boxes," recyclable
  cardboard/kraft paper, soy-based inks. Best fit for a small first drop.
- **BOWE PACK** (https://bowepack.com/collections/bar-packaging) — bar-wrapper
  specialist (central-seal and three-side-seal formats), compostable and
  recyclable materials.
- **Roastar** (https://www.roastar.com/custom-printed-candy-bags) — meets
  ASTM D6868 industrial-compostability standard.

## Two directions to choose between
1. **Minimal/kraft, Ffern-adjacent**: unbleached kraft paper wrapper, single
   ink color, small numbered-batch stamp — cheap, on-brand with the
   restraint-driven positioning, good MOQ fit for a first 30–50 unit
   hand-sell run (Eco Custom Packaging or Good Start Packaging).
2. **Premium/art-forward**: full-color printed compostable wrapper with
   original artwork per flavor or per drop, closer to Ffern's actual
   unboxing experience — higher cost and MOQ, better saved for Drop 01 once
   the product is validated (TIPA or BOWE PACK).

Recommendation for the hand-sell phase (30–50 units): start with option 1 —
low cost, low MOQ, keeps the $500 budget mostly available for cacao sourcing
and legal formation. Revisit option 2 for Drop 01 once there's real revenue.
