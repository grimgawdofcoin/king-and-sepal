# Product labels — first draft

Template labels built from what's locked in so far (name, flavors, Florida
cottage food requirements) plus the draft recipe in `RECIPE_NOTES.md`.
**Every bracketed placeholder below is not yet final** — ingredient order
will shift once the recipe is actually tested, and the address/net weight
need real values. Treat this as the label structure to fill in, not a
finished label.

## Required elements (Fla. Stat. § 500.80, cross-checked 2026-07-18/22)
Every package needs, in order:
1. Business name and address
2. Product name
3. Ingredients, in descending order by weight
4. Net weight or volume
5. Allergens
6. Verbatim disclaimer, ≥10-point type: *"Made in a cottage food operation
   that is not subject to Florida's food safety regulations."*

Since King & Sepal is candy (taxable — see `LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md` step 8),
there's no additional label requirement from that, but it's worth keeping
in mind that "candy" framing is also what makes the tax rule apply.
